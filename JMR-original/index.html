GAUNTLET: THE THIRD ENCOUNTER

How to play

Drag on the game picture to move. Tap the picture to fire. The D-pad also works.

Tap B once to hold B for inventory/scroll selection. B turns yellow. Use the D-pad, then tap B again to release.
