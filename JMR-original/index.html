How to play

Original: untouched original gameplay. You begin with 20,000 life points. Life slowly drains even while idle, and enemies drain it much faster. There is no ordinary lives counter.

Easy: the original ROM still runs unchanged, but the browser automatically fires A while you move, so you are much less likely to walk into enemies without attacking.

Woke (super easy): continuous rapid A-fire assist, whether moving or standing. You still need to navigate, use inventory, and avoid hazards, but combat is dramatically easier.

The cartridge is permanently built into this page. Tap PLAY GAUNTLET once after opening the page; you never select or upload a ROM.

Use A/B to advance opening screens, D-pad to move, OPTION 1/2 for the original option buttons, and PAUSE to pause. Keyboard: arrows, Z=A, X=B, Q/W=Options, Enter=Pause.
