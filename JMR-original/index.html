How to Play

Move: drag and hold on the game picture, or use the D-pad. Slide your finger to steer, including diagonals.

Fire: quick tap on the game picture or press A.

Inventory / scrolls: tap B once. B turns yellow and stays held. Use directions to scroll/select. Tap B again to release it.

OPTION 1, OPTION 2 and PAUSE remain normal Lynx buttons.
