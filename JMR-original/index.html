How to Play

Move: drag and hold anywhere on the game screen. Slide your finger to change direction. Diagonals work. You can also use the D-pad.

Fire: quick-tap the game screen, or use A/B. A movement drag never fires when you lift your finger.

Items: OPTION 1, OPTION 2, A, B and PAUSE remain explicit Lynx buttons.

Difficulty: Original is untouched. Easy auto-fires while moving. Woke adds continuous rapid fire.
