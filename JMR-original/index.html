Checking the original cartridge…

Your Lynx, in the browser

Tap PLAY GAUNTLET once. The cartridge is already inserted. There is nothing to upload or select. The first tap also lets the browser enable sound.

Use A or B to advance the opening screens. Use the D-pad to choose your character and the action buttons to confirm. During play, hold a direction to move and use A/B for the original game actions. You can hold a direction and an action together.

OPTION 1, OPTION 2 and PAUSE are the actual Lynx buttons. PAUSE pauses or resumes the original game. This game uses the Lynx screen vertically; the picture and directional controls are rotated together.

Keyboard: arrow keys move; Z = A, X = B; Q/W = Option 1/2; Enter = Pause. Reloading the page turns the console off and on; it does not require choosing a cartridge.
