Tap-fire assistance

These modes change the length of a deliberate tap-to-fire burst, not health or movement. No automatic firing.

Restart from the beginning? Current progress will be lost.

How to play

Start: tap A to advance the original title screens. Use directions to choose your character, then A.

Move: drag and hold on the picture. Steer in eight directions; lift to stop. The D-pad and arrow keys also work. Movement never fires.

Fire: tap the picture or press A. A second finger can tap while the first steers. A completed drag does not fire.

Items: tap B to hold it; yellow B ✓ means inventory mode is on. Use directions for the original item actions. Tap B again to release it. Firing is never automatic while using inventory.

Pause: MENU or PAUSE freezes the same session. Opening help or leaving the tab releases controls and B for safety. Resume does not restart.

Keyboard: arrows move, Z or Space fires, X toggles B, Q/W are Options, Enter opens the pause menu.
